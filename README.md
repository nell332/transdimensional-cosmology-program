# Transdimensional Energy Descent Cosmological Program

> 
> A Unified Cosmological Framework Based on Dimensional Interface Dynamics

## Overview

This work proposes a minimal-axiom unified cosmological research program, starting from 4 fundamental axioms, it interprets cosmic origin, mass-energy conversion, four fundamental interactions, quantum phenomena and dark matter effects through the core mechanism of higher-dimensional energy descending into 3D space and being retarded by collisions.

It is positioned as an ontological research program rather than a fully verified mature physical theory. It does not deny the computational validity of existing theories (relativity, quantum mechanics, ΛCDM) within their domains, and aims to provide an alternative path for fundamental physics research with fewer ad hoc assumptions.

## Core Framework

### Fundamental Axioms

1. **Temporal positivity and entropy increase principle**: Time is irreversible; entropy increase is the underlying constraint of energy-matter conversion.
2. **Layered dimensions and transdimensional migration**: The universe has a layered dimensional structure; energy can migrate across dimensions with an intrinsic interface velocity.
3. **3D space energy saturation threshold**: 3D space has a maximum carrying capacity per unit volume for descending energy; exceeding the threshold causes crowding collisions and velocity attenuation.
4. **Universal energy conservation**: Total energy of the universe is conserved, only transforming between forms and dimensional coupling states.

### Key Conclusions

- The speed of light c is the intrinsic interface velocity of higher-dimensional energy descending into 3D space.
- Mass is not an intrinsic static property, but a steady state of descending energy attenuated by collision drag.
- The Big Bang singularity is essentially a cosmic-scale white hole channel injecting 4D energy into 3D space.
- All four fundamental interactions are derivative effects of transdimensional tension at different scales.
- Quantum entanglement is the correlated projection of a single higher-dimensional entity in 3D space, naturally compatible with light-speed causality.

## Testable Predictions

1. Periodic modulation of quantum statistical distribution under ultra-high time-resolution measurement.
2. Spatial correlation between dark matter distribution and ancient black hole remnants; low dark matter abundance in cosmic voids.
3. Systematic deviations in primordial nucleosynthesis element abundance compared to the standard ΛCDM model.
